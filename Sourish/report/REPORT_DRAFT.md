### 2.3 Preprocessing

- Track rows sorted by timestamp; invalid coordinates, duplicate timestamps and single-point speed spikes (> 70 m/s to both neighbours) removed.
- Each trip projected to its own UTM zone (trips span several continents) so all distances are in metres.
- Road network cropped to the track bounding box + 600 m.
- Per-point labels created by monotone alignment of GPS points to the ordered route.
- Points farther than 50 m from their labelled route arc flagged unreliable.

## 5. HMM and Viterbi route reconstruction

Decoder parameters (tuned on 10 held-out training trips ['00000008', '00000010', '00000020', '00000039', '00000058', '00000068', '00000069', '00000082', '00000093', '00000096']; classifier refitted on the other 70): `{"emission": "classifier", "beta": 5.0, "emission_weight": 0.25, "max_penalty": 50.0, "sigma": 20.0}`; distance-only baseline: `{"emission": "distance", "beta": 20.0, "sigma": 20.0, "max_penalty": 50.0, "emission_weight": 1.0}`.

- Hidden states: candidate arcs of each GPS point.
- Emission: w · log(p / Σp) from the classifier (baseline: −½(d/σ)²).
- Transition: −|d_route − d_gps| / (β·√Δt), floored at −max_penalty; unreachable or backwards = −max_penalty.
- Points without candidates are skipped and forward-filled; long gaps (> 300 m) use a larger shortest-path search.
